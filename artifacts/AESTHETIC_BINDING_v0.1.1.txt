APPLICATION HOST
Android navigation application with one persistent 3D cartographic instrument.

RETAINED PRINCIPLES
- monolithic massing: 3D viewport is the dominant mass
- rectilinear authority: status, data recess, and four-screen selector are planar/rectilinear
- subtractive function: information surfaces read as integrated recesses, not decorative floating cards
- quiet interface: thin labels, exact state readouts, restrained lines
- grayscale base palette
- minimal functional signal only
- anti-ornamental behavior
- contained capability

HOST OVERRIDES / DEVIATIONS
- curves are permitted for true/data-derived trajectories, celestial geometry, uncertainty volumes, and touch gesture behavior because these are functional representations, not ornamental identity.
- Android accessibility/touch requirements take precedence over aesthetic compression where needed.

FOUR PRIMARY SCREENS
NOW = WHERE YOU ARE
HISTORY = WHERE YOU WERE
TARGET = WHERE YOU WANT TO GO
ROUTE = HOW TO GET THERE

SIGNAL ASSIGNMENT
WHITE = active/neutral cartographic state
AMBER = target, caution, degraded-but-usable, active route emphasis
RED = missing/critical/unusable state
All signal treatment remains subordinate to the scene.

EXCLUSIONS
No glassmorphism, neon-theater styling, fake holograms, ornamental gradients, playful iconography, ambient scanning effects, decorative star streaking, card-grid fragmentation, or fabricated celestial data.
